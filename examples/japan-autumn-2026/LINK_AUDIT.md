# Practical link audit — Japan, 2–13 November 2026

Audited on 8 September 2026 against every primary timeline, full scenario, and local event alternative. Named major visits and venues now carry an official or first-party page plus a Google Maps point where the place is fixed. Named rail and bus legs carry both an operator or official transport-directory page and a Google Maps route whenever the operator and endpoints are known. The itinerary contains 332 practical links on 159 event records: 160 official/ticket links, 119 map points, and 53 routes.

New official pages were opened only to confirm that each URL belongs to the named operator, venue, or public tourism authority. This link pass does not verify November timetables, prices, opening hours, ticket inventory, weather feasibility, or the underlying candidate claims. Existing claim and freshness statuses remain unchanged.
